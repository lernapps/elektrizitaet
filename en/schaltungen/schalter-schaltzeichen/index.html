The idea

If you want to write down a circuit, you do not have to draw cables and lamps. Every part has a short circuit symbol. That way people all over the world read the same diagram.

What to remember

Switch

A switch opens or closes the circuit. Closed, the current flows; open, none flows.

Circuit diagram

A circuit diagram shows a circuit with circuit symbols instead of drawings of the parts.

Circuit symbols and parts
PartHow to recognise the circuit symbol
Batterytwo bars, a long thin one and a short thick one
Lampa circle with a cross inside
Open switcha line with a gap; the current does not flow
Closed switchan unbroken line; the current flows

Example

A circuit diagram in words

A circuit diagram shows: battery, then a switch, then a lamp, then back to the battery. If the circuit symbol of the switch is unbroken, the switch is closed and the lamp lights up. If it shows a gap, the switch is open and the lamp stays dark.

Practice

Match each circuit symbol with the part.

Choose the match for each entry.

Right answer: a circle with a cross – Lamp; two bars, a long one and a short thick one – Battery; a line with a gap – open switch

The circle with a cross stands for the lamp, the two bars for the battery, and the gap in the line shows an open switch.

The lamp in a circuit with a switch is lit. What do you know about the switch?

  • It is closed. right answer

    Only when the switch is closed is the whole circuit closed, so that the current can flow.

  • It is open.

    With an open switch the circuit is broken and the lamp stays dark.

  • You cannot tell.

    Yes, you can. If the lamp is lit, the circuit must be closed everywhere, including at the switch.

The lamp only lights up when the current can flow through the switch. So it must be closed.
