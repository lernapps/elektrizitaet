The idea

Now you build for yourself. You get a task, for example “two lamps that shine separately”, and you put lamps and switches on the places of a circuit board. The battery is always there. In “Test” mode you flip switches and unscrew lamps to see what happens.

What to remember

  • Parts on the main wire (top and bottom left) are in series with everything else.
  • The two vertical branches form a parallel circuit. Each branch is a path of its own.
  • An empty place is just a wire. If one branch is without a lamp while the other has one, the current flows past the lamp branch: a short circuit. The builder warns you.

Example

Two lamps that can be switched separately

Put a switch and a lamp into each of the two vertical branches. Each switch only switches its own lamp.

Practice

Task: Build a circuit in which you switch a lamp on and off with a switch.

Choose a part and tap a place on the circuit board. With the wire you make a place empty again.

Lamps shining: 0

Short circuit! A wire without a lamp bypasses the lamps in the other branch, and the current flows past them.

These are the tasks the circuit builder sets, each with its solution. The circuit board has places in the main wire (1 and 6) and two branches with two places each (2 to 5):

  • One lamp shines: a lamp on place 1.
  • Lamp with a switch: a switch on place 1, a lamp on place 6.
  • Two lamps in series: lamps on places 1 and 6.
  • Two lamps in parallel: a lamp in each branch, for example on places 2 and 4.
  • Two lamps, each with its own switch: a switch and a lamp in each branch.
  • One switch for two parallel lamps: a switch on place 1, a lamp in each branch.

You put a lamp into the left vertical branch and leave the right branch empty, that is, without a lamp. What happens?

  • There is a short circuit, and the lamp does not shine. right answer

    The empty branch is a path for the current with no lamp at all. The current takes this path and leaves the lamp off.

  • The lamp shines more brightly.

    The lamp then gets no current at all, because the current takes the empty branch.

  • Nothing happens.

    An empty branch changes the circuit a lot. It is a short circuit.

A branch without a load is a short circuit. The current flows past every load, and that can make cables dangerously hot.
