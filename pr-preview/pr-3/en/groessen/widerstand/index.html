The idea

A thin, long wire makes it harder for the current than a short, thick one. In the water picture: less water flows through a long, narrow pipe than through a short, wide one. How strongly a part hinders the current is told by the resistance.

What to remember

Resistance

The electrical resistance says how strongly a part hinders the current. Its symbol is R and it is measured in ohms (Ω).

At the same voltage: the larger the resistance, the smaller the current.

What the resistance of a wire depends on
Change to the wireResistance
longergets larger
shortergets smaller
thickergets smaller
thinnergets larger
other materialcopper has a small resistance, constantan a large one

Resistor as a part

A part built in on purpose for its resistance is also called a resistor. Its circuit symbol is a rectangle.

Example

The long cable

Two copper cables are equally thick. One is 1 m long, the other 10 m. The long cable has the larger resistance. That is why less current gets through a cable drum that is fully unrolled.

Practice

Two copper wires are equally thick. One is 1 m long, the other 2 m. Which one has the larger resistance?

  • the 2 m one right answer

    The longer the wire, the larger the resistance.

  • the 1 m one

    The shorter wire has the smaller resistance.

  • both the same

    With the same material and thickness the length matters, and it differs.

The longer the wire, the larger the resistance.

What makes the resistance of a wire smaller?

  • a shorter wire right answer

    Less length, less resistance.

  • a thicker wire right answer

    A thick wire gives the current more room, so the resistance falls.

  • a longer wire

    A longer wire has a larger resistance.

  • a thinner wire

    A thinner wire has a larger resistance.

Shorter and thicker make the resistance smaller, longer and thinner make it larger.

Four copper wires are equally long and differ in thickness. Order them by their resistance, the largest first.

Give each entry its place: 1 for the first.

Right answer: 1. wire of 0.2 mm diameter, 2. wire of 0.5 mm diameter, 3. wire of 1 mm diameter, 4. wire of 2 mm diameter

The thinner the wire, the larger the resistance: the thinnest wire comes first.
